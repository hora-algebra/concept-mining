concept-mining

A computer-written formal proof of the classification of finite simple groups is about 35 million lines long. We rewrite it shorter while keeping the final theorem exactly as stated. A new definition or lemma that makes the whole proof shorter names an idea the proof keeps repeating; the more lines it saves, the more important the idea.

    The map

    Each rectangle is one part of the formalization. Parts are grouped by folder. Removed lines show up as hatched area inside the part they came from.
